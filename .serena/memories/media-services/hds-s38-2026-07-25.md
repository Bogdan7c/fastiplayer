## HDS container/probe invariant — 2026-08-05

- Любой accepted single-resource HDS candidate теперь нормализуется как `StreamLayout::ContentProbed` с provider-owned `probe_container = F4f`, независимо от полноты extractor codec metadata. Полные H.264/AAC hints сохраняются как `Declared` evidence и становятся exact planner decode requirements; `null` остаётся `Unknown`.
- F4F refinement допускается только когда каждый присутствующий extractor container hint относится к exact `Flv`/`F4f` family и присутствует хотя бы один такой hint. HDS с known Ogg/MP4 family либо со смешанным compatible+unknown hint отклоняется typed `InvalidMetadata { Container, ContradictoryHints }` до provider I/O; unknown-only сохраняет existing `UnknownContainer`. Transport boundary дополнительно требует exact `ContentProbed` role и F4F probe.
- App HDS composition принимает только этот единый contract. Поскольку worker-facing progressive demux до запуска может публиковать пустые tracks, authoritative track/codec/capability/hard-policy proof выполняет bounded eager rendition discovery до commit. Provider sibling outcomes разделены на content/profile `Rejected` и infrastructure `Unavailable`: parent fallback разрешён только для typed `HdsNoPlayableRendition` после all-Rejected pass; network/demux/bootstrap/cancellation failures не маскируются. Выбранный probed demuxer передаётся в runtime без второго initial fragment fetch-а.

- `hds-manifest-core` is I/O-free. It parses F4M 1.0/2.0 through the S04X `BoundedXmlReader`, requires the expected namespace for known schema elements, supports bounded hierarchy/baseURL/media/bootstrapInfo and rejects unsupported DRM/signature/cue/live-only shapes. Its Adobe `abst/asrt/afrt` boundary validates table counts before allocation, full-box/update flags, `afrt`/`abst` timescale agreement and discontinuity indicators. Timeline expansion takes the first fragment ID from `afrt`, accepts a legal final normal run without a terminal marker, maps the exact `asrt` fragment tail to Seg/Frag numbers and fails closed on unsupported non-terminal discontinuities.
- `web-media-hds` owns the S38 runtime. S31 `AdaptiveHttpContext` owns HTTP/cancel/generation/retry; S30 owns F4F/FLV demux and is reused through the existing `DemuxRegistry`. HDS creates `OrderedSegments` descriptors for F4F media fragments and wraps S30 with a transactional demux boundary for VOD seek at complete fragment anchors.
- Absolute HDS fragment timestamps are preserved instead of rewriting packet clocks. `web-media-hds::HdsVodPresentationWindow` publishes neutral absolute start/end bounds; app-egui maps them to player-owned `MediaPlaybackWindow`. `AdaptivePresentation::Vod` keeps relative presentation duration, while the demux boundary reports the absolute source end required to validate the bounded window. Public playback position therefore starts at zero even when the source bootstrap timestamp does not.
